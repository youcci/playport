A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Make yourself at home.

Connect your iPhone

  1. 01
    Open your personal viewer link

    Use the HTTPS link printed by the PlayPort server. It includes your access token.

  2. 02
    Pair with PlayPort over Bluetooth

    On your iPhone, open Settings → Bluetooth and select the server’s device name. If asked for a PIN, enter 3939.

  3. 03
    Let CarPlay take the wheel

    Keep the iPhone and server on the same Wi-Fi network. Accept the CarPlay prompt on your iPhone; the screen appears automatically.
